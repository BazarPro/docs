Kids' skis no longer fit after one winter, the ski boots pinch by the second, and in the basement there's a snowboard, ice skates and a ski jacket nobody wears anymore. At the same time, many families look for affordable gear before every season. A **ski swap** – also called a ski sale, winter sports sale or ski bazaar – brings both together: sellers drop off their gear, a team sells it on one day, and at the end everyone gets their money and their unsold items back.

A ski swap sits between a [bike sale](/en/ratgeber/fahrradboerse-organisieren/) and a [kids' bazaar](/en/ratgeber/kinderbasar-organisieren/): there are bulky, valuable items like skis and snowboards, but also many small ones like hats, gloves and goggles. This guide walks through a ski swap from the first idea to the cash count and shows how [BazarPro](https://bazarpro.de) takes lists, slips of paper and the recalculating in the evening off your hands.

<Aside type="tip" title="In short">
  A date before the first snow, a hall with enough room for ski racks, clear rules for bindings, helmets and pairs – and every item gets a QR label.
</Aside>

## Part 1: Planning

### The right date

A ski swap pays off **before the season**: late October to late November, when families put together gear for the winter and the school ski trip. A date shortly after the autumn holidays works well in many places. Check whether another ski swap in the region takes place on the same weekend – buyers travel far for good gear, but sellers get split up.

### The hall

Unlike a bike sale, a ski swap almost always takes place **indoors**: in a gym, a community hall or the clubhouse. Pay attention to:

- **Enough room to try things on.** Buyers try on ski boots and hold skis up to compare. Plan benches or chairs next to the sales areas.
- **Floor protection.** Ski boots and steel edges scratch gym floors. Ask the caretaker whether you need tarps, carpet tiles or mats.
- **Racks for skis and boards.** Skis stand best in ski racks or lean against fence panels, beer tables or gym equipment; snowboards lie flat on tables. Ask the ski club, the school or a sports shop for racks to borrow.
- **One entrance and exit** with the cash desk right next to it, so nothing leaves the hall unscanned.
- **Power** for intake and cash desk: printer, chargers, power banks.
- **Insurance:** Check whether your association's or school's liability insurance covers the event.

### Set three time slots

As with every bazaar, keep three phases clearly apart:

1. **Drop-off** – e.g. Friday 5:00–8:00 pm or Saturday 8:00–10:00 am.
2. **Sale** – e.g. Saturday 10:00 am–2:00 pm.
3. **Pickup and payout** – e.g. Saturday 4:00–5:00 pm.

Many ski swaps take in the gear **the evening before**. That gives you time to sort, and the morning starts on time. Enter drop-off and pickup in BazarPro in the **Hand-in and pickup** step. BazarPro then emails your sellers the evening before with drop-off time, venue and seller number, and reminds them to pick up after the sale.

### Create the bazaar in BazarPro

When creating it, you first choose the **type of bazaar**. For a ski swap, **Flea market or general bazaar** fits best: photos are optional, discounts, price offers and questions are possible. Then adjust a few settings:

- **Categories:** Choose the categories for winter sports, sports, shoes and clothing. Depending on the name, BazarPro asks for matching **extra fields** when an item is created:

  | Category contains | Extra fields |
  | --- | --- |
  | "Ski", "Snowboard" or "Sport" | Size / length (e.g. 160 cm), brand |
  | "Schuh" or "Stiefel" (shoe, boot), e.g. ski boots | Size (EU), brand |
  | "Jacke", "Hose" or "Kleid" (jacket, trousers, dress) | Size, brand |

  If a category you need is missing, contact the operator of your BazarPro instance.

- **Labels:** Set them to **Large** (12 per A4 page). Large labels attach well to skis, poles and boards with cable ties and can be read from a few metres away.
- **Items per seller** (optional), e.g. 20 – so intake and space are enough.
- **Commission and intake fee:** 10–20 % commission is common. A small **intake fee** per item, e.g. €1, keeps out shelf warmers and covers the hall rent. With 15 % commission and skis for €80, the seller gets €68 and €12 stay with you. With a €1 intake fee they get €67.

Optionally upload a **site map** and create locations such as "Adult skis", "Kids' skis", "Snowboards", "Ski boots" and "Clothing". Buyers then see where an item is when they scan it.

The step-by-step guide is in [Create a bazaar](/en/veranstalter/basar-anlegen/).

### Choose the features for your bazaar

| Feature | What it does at a ski swap |
| --- | --- |
| **Negotiating on site (minimum price)** | Skis and boards are often haggled over. Sellers set a minimum price buyers don't see, and the cash desk may go down to it. |
| **Discount items** | From a set time, e.g. 12:00, sellers lower their price – good for clothing and small items that would otherwise be left over. |
| **Online price offers** | Buyers offer a price by phone right at the item; the seller accepts or declines, even from home. |
| **Questions to the seller** | Buyers ask in advance, e.g. "How old is the binding?" or "What's the sole length of the boot?". |

<Aside type="caution" title="Discounts and the price on the label">
  **Print the price on the label** only works without discounts, because a printed price can't drop. With discounts, buyers see the current price when they scan the QR code.
</Aside>

### Set rules for sellers

Write the rules into the bazaar description. For a ski swap they include:

- **Winter sports only:** skis, snowboards, cross-country and touring gear, ski and snowboard boots, poles, helmets, goggles, ice skates, sledges and winter sports clothing. No everyday clothing.
- **Skis with bindings as one item.** Skis are dropped off **strapped together** in pairs with a velcro strap or ski clip, poles too. What belongs together gets one label.
- **Clean and working:** boots with liners, buckles intact, boards without broken edges.
- **No helmets after a crash.** A helmet that has taken a hard impact no longer protects reliably. Many swaps also only accept helmets younger than about five years.
- **Sold as seen:** Between private persons, warranty can be excluded. Remind buyers to check the gear before buying.
- **Uncollected items:** What happens to them after a deadline, for example a donation to the school's ski trip?

<Aside type="caution" title="Have the binding adjusted">
  The release settings of a ski binding have to match the new skier's weight, height, ability and boot. Only a specialist workshop may adjust them. State in the description that buyers should have used skis **checked and adjusted at a ski service** before the first run – and that you as the organizer don't adjust bindings.
</Aside>

The link to your bazaar in BazarPro is the central hub: sellers register through it, buyers see in advance which skis, boards and boots are on offer.

### More than just gear

A **ski service stand**, where a sports shop waxes skis or checks bindings, attracts visitors and helps buyers right away. Ask a sports shop nearby – many are happy to join because they win new customers. Add coffee, cake, kids' punch or mulled wine: it creates a good atmosphere and welcome income for the club.

## Part 2: Sellers get ready

Sellers register for the bazaar via the link and create their items from home – with photo, description and price. For each item BazarPro creates a **QR label** as a PDF with QR code, seller number, title and size.

For skis and boots to sell well, sellers should:

- give the **length** of skis and snowboards and the **size** of boots, because that's what buyers look for first. For ski boots, the **Mondopoint size** (e.g. 27.5) and the sole length in the description help as well.
- name **brand, model and age**, for skis also the binding model.
- **describe the condition honestly**: scratches in the base, state of the edges, last service.
- attach the label **with a cable tie** to the binding, pole or board, on boots to a buckle. For clothing a safety pin is enough.
- answer buyers' **questions** quickly. BazarPro sends an email for each one.

The guide for sellers: [List an item](/en/verkaeufer/artikel-inserieren/).

## Part 3: The bazaar day

### What to have ready

- **Smartphones or tablets** for intake and cash desk – BazarPro runs in the browser. You can also connect a **hand scanner** at the cash desk. Don't forget power banks.
- **A printer** with paper for forgotten labels.
- **Cable ties, velcro straps and scissors** for skis that arrive without a strap.
- **Ski racks, tables and floor protection.**
- **A tape measure** – buyers keep asking about length.
- **Change** and a lockable cash box.

### Assign helpers

In BazarPro you make participants **helpers** with one click. They can take in items, sell at the cash desk, settle and print labels – they can't change settings or delete the bazaar. Plan at least:

- **Intake:** two people, one scans, one carries the skis to their location.
- **Sales floor:** one or two people who put skis back and answer questions.
- **Cash desk:** better two at the peak right after opening.

### Intake

<Steps>

1. **Scan the label or ID.** In **Item management**, scan an item's label or the seller's **digital ID**. With the ID you see all registered items and accept them at once with **Accept all**.

2. **Check briefly.** Are the skis strapped together? Is the helmet undamaged, the liner included? Anything that doesn't meet your rules, you don't accept.

3. **Assign a location.** If you created locations, assign each item to an area, e.g. "Kids' skis". With an **internal note** you record details such as "Poles in the basket behind intake".

4. **Print missing labels.** With **Labels (PDF)** you print a seller's labels right at intake.

</Steps>

**Walk-in sellers without an account** are created under **Participants & helpers** via **Seller without account**, and you enter their items with **Add item**.

### The sale

- **Buyers inform themselves.** They scan the QR code with their phone camera and see photos, description, length or size, the current price and the location – without an app.
- **The cash desk scans.** If a family buys skis, poles, boots and a helmet, everything goes into **one cart**. With ONO, the cash desk chooses the negotiated price, which may not be below the minimum price. **Complete sale** sells everything, and every seller gets an email.
- **Receipt on request.** If the buyer gives their email address, they get one receipt for the whole purchase.

<Aside type="caution" title="Only one exit">
  Put the cash desk right at the exit. Skis and boards are easy to carry – and without a check, also easy to carry out unscanned.
</Aside>

### Pickup and payout

After the sale, BazarPro reminds sellers by email to pick up, with the payout amount and the list of their unsold items. At pickup you scan the **digital ID**, return the unsold items, pay out the proceeds minus commission and intake fee and click **Finish settlement**. The details are in [The bazaar day](/en/veranstalter/basartag/#settlement-and-return).

## Part 4: After the bazaar

In **Item management** you see which items are still **Available**, i.e. neither sold nor picked up. With **Message to sellers** and the option **Only sellers with items still to pick up** you reach everyone affected at once.

Finally, open the **cash count**: revenue, commission, intake fees and payouts at a glance, to compare with the cash in the box.

<Aside type="tip" title="For next year">
  For the next ski swap, simply take over this bazaar's settings when creating it, so you don't have to enter categories, fees and rules again.
</Aside>
