In this guide you join a bazaar as a seller, create an item and print its QR label. If you want to sell several items, just repeat the second part.

**You need:** a BazarPro account, photos of your items (if the bazaar asks for them) and – if the organizer uses one – the access code.

<Aside>
  The screenshots show the German version of the app.
</Aside>

## Part 1: Join the bazaar

<Steps>

1. **Switch to the “Participant” role.** At the bottom of the sidebar, under **Switch role**, click **Participant**.

2. **Open the bazaar.** Under **Events** in the sidebar you find all bazaars you can join. If the organizer sent you a link, just open it.

3. **Read the conditions.** In the sidebar of the bazaar page, **Fees & conditions** lists the commission, intake fee, item limit, drop-off and pickup times and what is allowed when selling – with a worked example of how much you get.

4. **Click “Join as a seller”.**

   ![Bazaar page with the buttons for viewing the items and joining as a seller](../../../../assets/screenshots/basar-seite-verkaeufer.png)

5. **Check your contact details.** The first time, BazarPro asks for your name, address and phone number so that the organizer can reach you. The details are saved in your profile.

   ![Registration dialog with fields for first name, last name and phone number](../../../../assets/screenshots/basar-beitreten-dialog.png)

6. **Enter the access code (if needed)** and finish with **Confirm registration**. If the bazaar only admits private sellers, you first confirm: “I sell as a private person, not commercially.”

</Steps>

After registering, you have a **seller number** at this bazaar, for example “No. 17”. It appears on your labels, on the bazaar page and in the emails.

<Aside type="caution" title="Registration doesn't work?">
  - **“The code is wrong!”** – check the code with the organizer; upper and lower case matter.
  - **Seller limit reached** – the bazaar already has as many sellers as the organizer allows.
  - **The event is in the past** – you can't register for bazaars that have ended.
</Aside>

## Part 2: Create items

<Steps>

1. **Open “New item”.** Under **My items**, click **New item** at the top right.

2. **Fill in the details.**

   ![Item form with name, description, condition, category, price, photos and event](../../../../assets/screenshots/artikel-formular.png)

   - **Item name** – at least 3 characters, e.g. “Dutch bike 28 inch”.
   - **Description** – optional but helpful: age, signs of wear, accessories.
   - **Condition** – New, Used like new, Used or Used with defects.
   - **Category** – the bazaar only accepts the categories the organizer allowed. Depending on the category, BazarPro asks for more details, such as **size** and **brand** for clothes or the **frame number** for bikes. Fields marked `*` are required by the bazaar.
   - **Price (€)** – your sale price. The organizer's commission is deducted from it.
   - **Minimum price** (optional) – only at bazaars with negotiating on site. Buyers see “negotiable” but not the minimum price.
   - **Photos** – at most bazaars at least one photo, at some optional.
   - **Offered at event** – pick the bazaar. You can also assign an item to several bazaars or do it later.

3. **Save.** **Create item** and you're done. **Create & add another** goes straight to the next item – bazaar, category and condition stay set. The item is now listed at the bazaar with the status **Registered**.

</Steps>

If the organizer limits the **items per seller**, BazarPro tells you how many you can still register.

**Adding existing items to a bazaar:** Under **My items**, mark items with **Multiple selection** and choose **Add to event**. If an item lacks something the bazaar requires – a photo or a required detail – BazarPro walks you through those items one by one.

<Aside type="tip" title="Good photos sell better">
  Take photos in daylight against a calm background and show the whole item. A second photo of defects or details builds trust – and saves questions at the bazaar.
</Aside>

## Part 3: Print and attach labels

![“My items” page with item cards and “QR code” buttons](../../../../assets/screenshots/meine-produkte.png)

- **Single label:** click **QR code** on the item card.
- **All labels at once:** mark items with **Multiple selection** and download **Labels as PDF**.

The label shows the QR code, title, your seller number and the size. The organizer sets the format: large labels (12 per page) or small ones (40 per page) to cut out. BazarPro only prints the price if the bazaar is set up that way.

Print the labels and attach them where they're easy to see – to clothes with a safety pin, for example, or to a bike's handlebar. Then bring the items to the bazaar's intake.

## What happens next?

- **The evening before at 6 pm** you get an email with the drop-off time, location, your seller number and the number of your items.
- **Until intake** you can edit or delete the item. If the bazaar keeps a record, title, category, price and required details are fixed from registration, and you can only withdraw items until the bazaar starts.
- **From intake on**, the item is **Available** and locked for changes.
- **During the bazaar** you can give an available item a **discount** if the bazaar allows it – at most up to the organizer's maximum discount. You can't take the discount back for the rest of the bazaar.
- **After the sale** you see the status **Sold** in BazarPro immediately and get an email. After the bazaar, BazarPro reminds you to pick up your money and unsold items.

**Messages from the organizer** appear on the bazaar page. BazarPro marks new messages at **Events** in the menu.

## Which emails do I get?

Under **Account → Notifications** you can turn off single kinds of email: sales, price offers, questions, your own offers and questions as a buyer, bazaar reminders and messages from the organizer. Each of these emails also has an unsubscribe link. Receipts and notices about your account always arrive, as do messages the organizer marks as **important**.

You get emails in the language you set in BazarPro – German or English.
