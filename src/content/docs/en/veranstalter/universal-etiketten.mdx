At the end of a kids' or clothing bazaar, hundreds of small items lie on the tables and have to go back to their sellers. **Universal labels** make this easier: instead of a number, the label has a mark made of a large digit, colors and symbols. Helpers recognize it at a glance and don't have to read anything.

The mark belongs to the seller and never changes. So their labels also work at other bazaars with universal labels, and they don't have to print them again.

<Image src={etiketten} alt="Eight small labels with QR code, title and size. Four of them carry the same mark: a large 5 with a yellow field with a diamond, a blue one with a circle and a black one with bars. The others show 4 A and 3 with other fields." />

## Which label suits my bazaar?

Under **Seller on the label** you choose how your team tells the items apart. The QR code is always there and works the same with all three options.

| Option | Sorting back | At the next bazaar | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Seller number of this bazaar** | Read the number | Print again, the number is only valid for this bazaar | Few items or large labels, e.g. bikes |
| **Universal label** | At a glance, no reading | Reuse at bazaars with universal labels | Many small items, e.g. clothes and toys |
| **Nothing, QR code only** | Scan every item | Reuse | Few items that don't need sorting |

## Set it up

The templates **Children's bazaar** and **Clothing & second-hand bazaar** already use universal labels. You don't have to do anything.

For other bazaars, set it in the step **Which items?**, or later under **Edit settings**:

<Steps>

1. Under **Labels**, choose the format **Small** (40 per A4 page). The large format always prints the seller number.

2. Under **Seller on the label**, choose the **Universal label**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Decide before printing">
  A later change only applies to newly printed labels. So decide before your sellers print.
</Aside>

## How the mark is built

- **Large digit:** you use it to split the items into ten piles first.
- **Three fields:** a wide one on top and two small ones below. Each has one of six colors, and each color has its own symbol: circle, triangle, square, diamond, cross or bars.
- **Letter (rare):** only after 2160 sellers does a letter appear below the digit, so that every mark stays unique.

The symbols keep the mark clear **in black-and-white printing** and for color-blind helpers:

## Sorting items back

<Image src={sortierblatt} alt="Sorting sheet with the mark in large format, plus the name Anna Beispiel and No. 17" />

<Steps>

1. **Print the sorting sheets.** Under **Participants & helpers**, **Sorting sheets** downloads a PDF. It has one sheet per seller with a large mark, the name and the seller number, already in the right order.

2. **Set up the baskets.** Put the matching sheet in each basket and line the baskets up in this order.

3. **Pre-sort by digit.** Put the unsold items on ten piles by their large digit.

4. **Sort them in.** Take a pile and put each item in the basket with the same colors and symbols.

</Steps>

You also see each seller's mark in the list under **Participants & helpers**. Settlement works as usual, see [Settlement and return](/en/veranstalter/basartag/#settlement-and-return).

## Reusing labels

The QR code leads to the item, not to the bazaar. If an item stays unsold, the label can simply stay on:

<Steps>

1. Under **My items**, the seller assigns the item to the next bazaar with **Add to event**.

2. If the new bazaar also uses universal labels, they bring the item with its old label to intake. It's scanned there like any other.

</Steps>

A label always belongs to **exactly one item**. For a new item, the seller prints a new one. To avoid wasting sheets, they can enter where to continue on a partly used sheet under **First free place on the sheet** when printing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there still a seller number?** Yes. It just isn't on the label anymore. It stays in the search, in the emails and on the sorting sheets.

**Does anything change at the cash desk?** No. Intake and the cash desk scan the QR code as always.

**Can a seller change their mark?** No, on purpose: that's the only way their printed labels stay valid.

**Does the mark also work on other servers?** No. Marks and QR codes are valid within one BazarPro installation. If you [self-host](/en/selbst-hosten/), you have your own marks, and labels from bazarpro.de don't work there.
